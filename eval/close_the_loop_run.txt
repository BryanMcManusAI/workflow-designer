ADJUDICATOR FIXED 2026-09-21 — AND THE HEADLINE ABOVE DOES NOT SURVIVE IT

`answer --majority N` is an ABSOLUTE threshold, so across arms of different depth it is not one
standard: 2 of 3 passes is 67% of the evidence, 2 of 6 is 33%, and 3 of 3 is unsatisfiable on a
disputed item. A tiered or audit design spends unevenly BY CONSTRUCTION, so no fixed N is fair to
both arms. eval/proportional_answer.py resolves a unit when strictly more than half of THAT ITEM'S
own passes share a reading — 3 needs 2, 4 needs 3, 6 needs 4 — so the standard is identical at every
depth. All four arms re-released on it:

  arm                              recall    FA  PPV@  NPV@  rejects       adjudicate
  loop2/arm_a_human_audit_sample      82%   14%   39%   97%    False      29.2 -> 29.2%
  loop2/arm_b_flat                    82%   17%   34%   97%    False      26.4 -> 26.4%
  loop3/arm_a_gold_honeypots          80%   20%   14%   98%    False      11.8 -> 11.8%
  loop3/arm_b_flat                    82%   18%   34%   96%    False      27.3 -> 27.3%

RETRACTED: "THE ENGINE'S RECOMMENDATION LOST TO THE PATH NOT TAKEN." It did not. That claim rested
entirely on a gate flip — arm B earning authority while arm A did not — measured at `--majority 3`,
which arm A's depth could not satisfy. Under one standard NO arm earns authority, and on the numbers
the engine's pick is if anything the better of the two: same recall, lower false alarms (14% vs 17%),
higher projected PPV (39% vs 34%).

WHAT IS LEFT, WHICH IS WEAKER AND TRUE:
  - The gate does not discriminate these arms on this pool. Nothing earns authority, so it cannot
    rank them. An instrument that returns the same verdict for every arm is not measuring the arms.
  - ADJUDICATION LOAD still discriminates, and sharply: the abstention-driven pick carries 11.8%
    against flat's 27.3%. Screening out the two worst raters more than halves what a person must
    settle. That is a real difference and it survives the fix.
  - So the loop's usable output right now is the human-load column, not the gate verdict.
